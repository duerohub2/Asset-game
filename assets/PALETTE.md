# Virtual City — Palette & Visual Rules

- **Grass / parks:** fresh greens, a few warm flower accents.
- **Roads:** blue-grey asphalt, muted yellow lane marks.
- **Paving:** desaturated stone grey with visible seams.
- **Buildings:** warm terracotta, cream plaster, teal windows, varied roofs.
- **Nature:** layered leafy silhouettes, brown trunks, simple highlights.
- **Characters:** compact chibi proportions, dark outlines, high-contrast clothing colors.
- **UI:** parchment cream panels, muted teal, warm red accents and clear dark strokes.

Keep shapes readable at small scale. Use hard edges and avoid texture noise. The intended direction is an original cozy isometric city, inspired by broad retro RPG readability rather than copying any named game's sprites or maps.
